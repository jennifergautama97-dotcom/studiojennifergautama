Lamplight

Project type
Residential - HDB resale
Location
Singapore
Scope
Interior styling
Expected completion
End of November 2026

A resale flat restyled rather than rebuilt - the right pieces in the right places. Light oak, bentwood and soft linen warm up the existing marble floors and curved cornices, while layered lamps do what the ceiling lights can't, pooling a low, golden glow from corner to corner. A round table anchors the room as a place to eat, work and linger, and a deep green sofa with a pop of orange keeps it from feeling too precious. A home that comes into its own once the sun goes down.
