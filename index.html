What you ask for isn't always what you need.

Hey! I'm Jennifer - architecture and interior trained, with experience spanning large-scale developments down to residential interiors. Everyone's taste is different, and my job is turning yours into something real; getting it right matters more to me than getting it done fast. I'm a little obsessed with the gap between what people ask for and what they actually need.

I want to create spaces that aren't just beautiful, but functional - and, most importantly, an extension of the people who live in them every day.

Outside work, you'll find me climbing, reading, or sketching by hand. Reach out anytime - I'd love to build something beautiful together.
