Held Over

Project type
Residential - BTO
Location
822 Keat Hong Grange, Singapore
Scope
Interior design + project management
Expected completion
December 2026 (key collection)

The homeowners were torn between two loves: the quiet elegance of a Parisian apartment, and the warmth of real wood. Held Over is what happens when you stop choosing. Arches soften the walls and glow from within. Panelled doors and slim brass pulls bring the French manners; oak and walnut bring the warmth, pale in the kitchen and deep at the dining room door. Herringbone runs underfoot the whole way through.

The idea was a home that feels as if it has been lived in and loved for years - held over, not made over - even on the day the keys are handed over.
